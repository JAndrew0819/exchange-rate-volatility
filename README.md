# Does exchange rate volatility drive macroeconomic volatility?



Replication of Itskhoki and Mukhin (2021) for Germany. The end of Bretton Woods
in the early 1970s is used as a natural experiment, since exchange rates shifted
abruptly from fixed to floating while other economic conditions evolved more
gradually. Extended to exchange rate levels and the UK's ERM experience.
